Frequently Asked Questions
How do I track my job applications status?
Go to the 'My Applications' page from the left sidebar to view real-time status updates (Under Review, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, etc.) for all your applied positions.
How can I update my default resume?
Navigate to settings page, look for the 'Resume Preferences' panel, click the Edit option, and upload your updated PDF resume document.
Why is my resume score not increasing?
Complete missing account profile criteria (e.g., preferred locations, skills badges, experience details) to boost your profile score metrics.
